Hello, I am Zachary. Eformics Systems builds software and websites that businesses actually use. We have been doing it since 2009, from Mississauga, Ontario.

Here is how we work. Most of what we do is build things for our clients — custom software, websites, and progressive web apps. Let me walk through each one.

First, custom development. Every business runs a little differently, and off-the-shelf tools often do not fit. So we build applications around the way your business actually works. Customer portals. Internal tools. Booking systems. Reporting dashboards. Whatever takes the slow, repetitive work off your team's plate. We start by learning your process. Then we build software that matches it.

Second, website design. A website should do more than look nice. We build sites that load fast, work on any screen, and are built to turn visitors into leads. Clear layout. Clear calls to action. The search basics done right from the start. And the same team stays on to support and improve the site after it goes live.

Third, progressive web apps. A progressive web app is a website that works like an app. People can install it on their phone or computer. It keeps working on a weak connection, or no connection at all. And there is no app store to deal with. For a small or mid-sized business, it is a simple, low-cost way to give customers an app experience — without paying to build separate apps for iPhone and Android.

Practical over flashy, every time.
